## What counts as a conversation?

A conversation is one user session that includes at least one user message and one assistant reply. Follow-up messages in the same session do not create a new conversation until the session expires (idle for 30 minutes) or the user starts a new chat.

## Free trial

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start demo mode. During trial you get Growth features capped at 500 conversations.

## Add-ons

- **SSO (SAML/OIDC):** +$99 / month on Growth; included on Scale.
- **Priority indexing:** same-day re-index after doc uploads; included on Scale.
- **Human handoff widget:** routes unanswered questions to Zendesk or Intercom; +$49 / month.

## Billing & taxes

Subscriptions renew monthly on the signup anniversary. Annual prepay saves 20%. Prices exclude applicable sales tax / VAT.
