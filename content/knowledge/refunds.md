# Acme Support AI — Refunds & Cancellations

## Money-back guarantee

New customers on Starter or Growth may request a full refund within **14 days** of the first paid charge if they are not satisfied. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## How to request a refund

1. Email billing@acme-support-ai.example from the account owner address.
2. Include your workspace ID and the reason for the request.
3. Cancel the subscription in **Settings → Billing** so you are not charged again.

## Mid-cycle cancellations

You can cancel anytime. Access continues until the end of the current billing period. We do **not** prorate unused days after the 14-day guarantee window, except where required by local law.

## Chargebacks

If you open a chargeback before contacting billing, we may freeze the workspace until the dispute is resolved. Please email us first — we resolve most billing issues within one business day.

## Free trial cancellations

Cancel anytime during the trial with no charge. Unused trial conversations do not roll over after conversion to a paid plan.

## Exceptions

Refunds are not available for:
- Scale / custom enterprise contracts (governed by the signed MSA)
- Usage already consumed after the 14-day window
- Accounts terminated for abuse or ToS violations
